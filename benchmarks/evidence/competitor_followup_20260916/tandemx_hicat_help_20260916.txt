usage: HiCAT_human.py [-h]
                      {reads,assembly,reads_aggregate,assembly_match,only_reads,reads_with_assembly}
                      ...

automatically annotate centromere HOR patterns from reads and assemblies

positional arguments:
  {reads,assembly,reads_aggregate,assembly_match,only_reads,reads_with_assembly}
                        Modes: reads,assembly,reads_aggregate,assembly_match,o
                        nly_reads,reads_with_assembly
    reads               Annotate centromere HOR patterns from reads
    assembly            Annotate centromere HOR patterns from assembly
    reads_aggregate     Merge the reads HOR annotation results for multi-
                        samples, and plot the normalized HOR number change
                        graph among samples
    assembly_match      Match the assembly HOR annotation results for multi-
                        samples based on reads annotation results
    only_reads          Reads annotation and aggregate
    reads_with_assembly
                        Annotation for reads and assemblies, aggregate the
                        results

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
