# Figure 4: preferential recovery in improved assemblies

This source-linked manuscript candidate uses only the frozen primary
family-metrics and summaries for Ey15-2 and Macadamia. It presents the
historical-to-newer assembly comparison as a family-level reference-proxy test:
the newer assemblies are not absolute biological copy-number truth.

Panels B and D show historical versus newer representation for every eligible
family. Panels C and E retain the observed relationship between the HiFi-to-old
deficit and the newer-to-old assembly gain, while explicitly keeping magnitude
agreement descriptive. Panel F reports the 8/8 and 2/2 primary-set matches
without presenting them as population accuracy.
