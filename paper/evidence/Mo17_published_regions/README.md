# Original Mo17 supplement and extracted region evidence

Chen et al., Nature Genetics (2023), [doi:10.1038/s41588-023-01419-6](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-023-01419-6),
CC-BY-4.0. The original XLSX is unmodified. The TSV is our extraction, with
source cells, source SHA256, NCBI contig mapping and explicit coordinate inference.

Contains 64 satellite regions, two rDNA regions, ten CENH3-defined centromeres
and 20 telomeres. Mixed-region percentages must not become base-level repeat
truth. Same-reference numeric coordinate agreement does not prove independent
assembly truth or same-donor identity. See
[extraction and reuse documentation](../../../docs/published_mo17_regions.md).
Two independent readers agree on the 2,033 selected source cells.
