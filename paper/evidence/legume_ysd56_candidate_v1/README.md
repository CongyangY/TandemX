# YSD56 785-bp tandem-array candidate evidence

This compact package records the retained evidence for the YSD56 candidate first
named `TXF000708` at 1.0897x and matched to `TXF000598` at nested 10.9570x.
The complete native discovery catalogues, read inputs and profiler samples remain
on `/Volumes/T7/Codex/TandemX`.

## Supported conclusions

- The 785-bp representative recurs exactly in the nested deeper HiFi catalogue
  (zero edits; 97 deeper supporting reads; 1,397,702 bp supporting span).
- TRF 4.10.0-rc.2 and TideHunter 1.5.5 each cover the full 124,029-bp assembly
  interval and independently report dominant period 785 bp.
- The candidate has no qualifying hit in the fixed local soybean-clone,
  supplemental soybean/wild-soybean, rDNA, organelle, soybean-minisatellite or
  Dfam *Glycine max* screens represented here.
- The YSD56 source article and its official supplement do not report a 785-bp
  family.

## Boundary

The deeper sample contains the pilot sample and is not independent replication.
Assembly detector agreement confirms tandem structure in the assembly, not
physical copy number, HOR structure, centromeric function or novelty.
`CentGm-2`, `CentGm273` and `CentGm444` lack directly retrieved monomer
sequences in the current audit. No independent ONT/Illumina support, full
nucleotide-database search or wet-lab validation has completed. The retained
status is `stable_previously_unreported_in_source_article_candidate`, not
`novel_TR`.

Coordinates in `TXF000708_array.fasta` are zero-based half-open and derive from
`GCA_040083835.1` sequence `CP154579.1:13,966,881-14,090,910`.
